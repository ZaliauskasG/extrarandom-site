InstructionsSURVIVING A SHIFT

STAND ON THE PAD
That's the whole game. I.T. stand on renovation zones to build; employees stand on fault stations to break. No button — just standing.
BRING FRIENDS
More people on a pad, faster work. Alone on a two-person job is 3× slower. Sabotage progress is shared, so joining a job picks up where they left off — but walk away and it's lost.
I.T.: FIX THEN BUILD
Stand on a fault 3 seconds to clear it. Leave it and Schelle emails. With 3 faults open renovation stops dead. 3 emails and you lose the afternoon.
GETTING CAUGHT
Saboteurs glow with a red ! and stay catchable 2s after stepping off. A catch takes the whole crew. Third catch and HR writes you up for good. Teammates can spring you early by standing in Schelle's office.
TOOLS
I.T. — PING sweeps the cameras and reveals every employee (10s, dead while Cameras Offline). EMPLOYEES — SMALL TALK traps nearby I.T. in conversation for 2s: they cannot move, cannot catch, can only nod. MAP shows all three floors, your team only. T messages your team.
HOW IT ENDS
I.T. win by finishing all 5 renovations, or getting every employee written up. Employees win by running out the clock, or 3 emails from Schelle.

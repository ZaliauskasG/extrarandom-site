    How it works

    1. Scan everyone's sheets into one PDF and choose it above. It doesn't leave your computer; all the reading happens right here in the browser.
    2. Press Convert and leave the tab open while the ball makes its way down the field.
    3. Download the spreadsheet. Cells marked orange need a look: open the Review tab, where each one is listed next to a picture of that spot on the sheet.
    4. Fix anything flagged, then copy the rows into the pool spreadsheet. Fill in the Winner row and the Wins column counts itself.

    The week number comes from the file name, so name the PDF something like "NFL Week 4 Picks". Names are matched against the list of regulars; add new players with Edit regulars.

    Regulars

    One player per line, the way they appear in the spreadsheet: first name, then last initial (for example "Mike M").
